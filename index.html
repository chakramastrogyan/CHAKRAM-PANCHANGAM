Vaidic Muhurtham System

Auspicious & Inauspicious Muhurtham Timings as per Vedic Scriptures

1. Brahma Muhurtham (బ్రహ్మ ముహూర్తం)

Timings: 04:42 AM - 05:30 AM

Vedic Significance: Occurs during the 14th Muhurtham period of the night (approx. 1 hour 36 minutes before sunrise). Presided over by Lord Brahma. Ideal for spiritual awakening, Veda recitation, yoga, and meditation.

2. Abhijit Muhurtham (అభిజిత్ ముహూర్తం)

Timings: 11:45 AM - 12:33 PM

Vedic Significance: The 8th Muhurtham of the daytime precisely around solar noon. Lord Vishnu’s Sudarshana Chakra destroys all doshas during this period. Excellent for initiating business, journeys, and crucial tasks.

3. Vijaya Muhurtham (విజయ ముహూర్తం)

Timings: 02:08 PM - 02:56 PM

Vedic Significance: The 11th Muhurtham of daytime granting victory ("Vijaya"). Favored for launching litigations, competitive exams, sports, and strategic ventures.

4. Godhuli Muhurtham (గోధూళి ముహూర్తం)

Timings: 05:30 PM - 05:54 PM

Vedic Significance: Dusk window when cows return home raising holy dust ("Go-Dhuli"). Goddess Lakshmi resides in this transition; highly recommended for Deeparadhana and weddings.

5. Nishitha Kalam (నిశీథ కాలం)

Timings: 11:40 PM - 12:28 AM

Vedic Significance: Midnight hour dedicated to Lord Shiva and Goddess Kali Sadhana. Highly auspicious for Shivaratri Abhishekam and Tantric spiritual practices.

1. Rahu Kalam (రాహు కాలం)

Timings: 03:00 PM - 04:30 PM

Why to Avoid: Shadow planet Rahu eclipses positive energies during this 90-minute window. Starting new ventures during Rahu Kalam leads to obstacles and delays.

2. Yama Gandam (యమ గండం)

Timings: 09:00 AM - 10:30 AM

Why to Avoid: Ruled by Lord Yama (God of Death). Important travels or auspicious ceremonies initiated during Yamagandam face destruction or loss.

3. Gulika Kalam (గుళిక కాలం)

Timings: 12:00 PM - 01:30 PM

Why to Avoid: Ruled by Saturn's son Gulika. Any action performed during this time gets repeated; avoid executing evil or sad rituals during Gulika Kalam.

4. Dhurmurtham (దుర్ముహూర్తం)

Timings: 08:32 AM - 09:20 AM

Why to Avoid: Inauspicious Muhurtham period causing conflicts, miscommunications, and failure of auspicious events.

5. Varjyam (వర్జ్యం)

Timings: 08:15 PM - 09:45 PM

Why to Avoid: Toxic star influence window ("Vishaghati"). Strictly forbidden for marriages, house warming (Griha Pravesham), and medical treatments.
